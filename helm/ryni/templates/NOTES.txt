5. Data persistence:
   - PVC "{{ include "ryni.pocketbase.fullname" . }}" is annotated
     with `helm.sh/resource-policy: keep`. `helm uninstall` does NOT
     delete it. Storage retention across PVC deletion is controlled by
     the StorageClass's `reclaimPolicy` (set it to `Retain` to keep
     the underlying PV too).
   - The Namespace itself is also annotated with
     `helm.sh/resource-policy: keep`. Helm will never delete it;
     remove it by hand with `kubectl delete namespace ...` when you
     really want the cluster back.

6. Reset (start over):
   To wipe the PB data, drop the PVC (the Secret is independent and can be
   re-read):
     kubectl delete pvc -n {{ .Values.namespace.name | default .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "ryni.pocketbase.fullname" . }}
   The next pod start re-creates an empty pb_data directory and re-creates
   the superuser from the Secret.
